Flashing Instruction Using crDroid Recovery

  2. Reboot recovery
  3. Flash copypartition-T812.zip
  4. Reboot recovery
  5. Format Data
  6. Flash Rom
  7. Reboot recovery (optional)
  8. Flash GAPPS (optional)
  9. Reboot system
