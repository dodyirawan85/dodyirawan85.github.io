Prerequisites

  1. Unlocked Bootloader

Flashing Instruction Using AOSP Recovery

  1. Flash boot, dtbo and vendor_boot
    fastboot flash --slot=all boot boot.img
fastboot flash --slot=all dtbo dtbo.img
fastboot flash --slot=all vendor_boot vendor_boot_userdebug.img
fastboot reboot recovery
  2. Flash V898 Firmware
  3. Reboot Recovery
  4. Format Data
  5. Flash Rom
  6. Reboot Recovery (optional)
  7. Flash GAPPS (optional)
  8. Reboot system
