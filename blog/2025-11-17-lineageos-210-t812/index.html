Prerequisites

  1. Unlocked Bootloader

Flashing Instruction Using LineageOS Recovery

  1. Flash boot, dtbo, vendor_boot
    fastboot flash boot boot.img
fastboot flash dtbo dtbo.img
fastboot flash vendor_boot vendor_boot.img
  2. Reboot recovery
  3. Flash copypartition-T812.zip
  4. Reboot recovery
  5. Format Data
  6. Flash Rom
  7. Reboot recovery (optional)
  8. Flash GAPPS (optional)
  9. Reboot system
